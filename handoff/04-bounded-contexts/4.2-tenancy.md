# 4.2 Tenancy

The Tenancy context owns the platform's tenant hierarchy: **Organizations** at the top, containing one or more **Stores**, with **Users** joining Organizations as Members and being assigned as Store Admins of specific stores within their Org.

> Backed by: [ADR-0003](../../decisions/0003-store-lifecycle-and-state-machine.md) (Store lifecycle), [ADR-0010](../../decisions/0010-invitation-account-reconciliation.md) (Invitation reconciliation), [ADR-0018](../../decisions/0018-organization-tenancy.md) (Organization expansion), [ADR-0019](../../decisions/0019-manual-catalog-republication.md) (manual republish).
> Authorization is layered on top of Tenancy — see [ADR-0002](../../decisions/0002-authorization-tiers-and-matrix.md) and [ADR-0016](../../decisions/0016-authorization-details.md), plus [§5.1](../05-cross-cutting.md) (forthcoming).
> Full entity model: [`design/tenancy.md`](../../design/tenancy.md).

## Position and ownership

**Owns:**
- `Organization` (top-level tenant; platform-internal; invisible to Beckn)
- `OrganizationMember` (`User` ↔ `Organization` with role: `Owner` | `Member`)
- `OrganizationInvitation` (email-targeted invitation to join an Org)
- `Store` (within an Org)
- `StoreAdminAssignment` (`User` ↔ `Store` — Org Member promoted to Store Admin)

**Consumes:**
- `User.id` from Identity & Access (for OrganizationMember, StoreAdminAssignment, audit actors).
- `Authorization` port from the Tenancy context itself (Tenancy provides the matrix-evaluation logic that all contexts call).

**Is consumed by:**
- **Catalog**, **Inventory**, **Promotion**, **Order** — each references `Store.id`.
- **Beckn Bridge** — projects each Active Store as a `Provider` on the network (per [§3.2](../03-beckn-integration.md)). The Bridge has no knowledge of Organizations.
- **Audit** — subscribes to Tenancy events.

## The tenancy hierarchy (per [ADR-0018](../../decisions/0018-organization-tenancy.md))

```
Organization (top-level tenant; platform-internal)
    │
    ├── owns 1..N Stores                  (each Store projects to Beckn as a Provider)
    │
    └── has 1..N OrganizationMembers
              │
              └── role = Owner            (exactly one per Org; plenary authority)
              │
              └── role = Member           (joined via Org Invitation)
                      │
                      └── 0..N StoreAdminAssignments   (assigned by Org Owner to specific Stores)
```

- A **User** can belong to **multiple Organizations** (multi-Org membership is supported).
- A **Store** belongs to **exactly one Organization** (immutable; no cross-Org store transfer in v1).
- **Org Owner** has implicit Store Admin authority over every store in their Organization — no `StoreAdminAssignment` row needed.
- **Org Members** (non-Owners) need an explicit `StoreAdminAssignment` to act on a specific store.

## Key concepts

### Organization

- `id`, `slug` (URL-safe, unique within platform, immutable)
- `name`, `description` — `LocalizedText`
- `status` — `Active` | `Suspended`
- `supported_locales`
- Timestamps

Organizations are **platform-internal** — they don't project to Beckn. Stores within an Org each project independently as a `Provider`.

### OrganizationMember

- `organization_id`, `user_id`
- `role` — `Owner` | `Member` (exactly one Owner per Org at all times)
- `status` — `Active` | `Removed` (retained for audit)

### OrganizationInvitation

Same shape as the original Invitation entity from [ADR-0010](../../decisions/0010-invitation-account-reconciliation.md), but now targets an Org (not a Store):

- `organization_id`, `email`, `intended_role` (always `Member` in v1)
- `token`, `issued_at`, `expires_at`
- `state` — `Pending` | `Accepted` | `Declined` | `Revoked` | `Expired`

Acceptance creates an `OrganizationMember`; the reconciliation rules (IdP-verified email match, email verification gate, idempotency) from ADR-0010 apply unchanged.

### Store

- `id`, `slug` (unique within Org), `organization_id` (immutable)
- `name`, `description` — `LocalizedText`
- `currency` (immutable after first Active product, per [ADR-0007](../../decisions/0007-pricing-tax-and-vouchers.md))
- `supported_locales`
- `status` — `Draft` | `Active` | `Suspended` | `Paused` (per ADR-0003)

**Change from earlier model**: no per-store `Owner` — ownership is at the Organization level (per ADR-0018).

### StoreAdminAssignment

Links an `OrganizationMember` to a specific `Store` within the same Org, granting Store Admin role for that store.

- `store_id`, `user_id` (must be an Active Org Member of the Store's Org)
- `assigned_at`, `assigned_by_user_id`
- `status` — `Active` | `Removed`

The Org Owner does NOT need (and cannot have) a `StoreAdminAssignment` — their Owner role grants implicit access.

## Lifecycles

### Organization

| From → To | Actor |
|---|---|
| (new) → `Active` | Any authenticated, email-verified User. Creator becomes the Org Owner. |
| `Active` → `Suspended` | Platform (capability `org.suspend`). Cascades: all stores in Org → `Suspended`. |
| `Suspended` → `Active` | Platform. Stores remain `Suspended` — explicit per-store reactivation needed. |

No `Draft` (Orgs don't publish to Beckn). No `Paused` (no buyers to hide from). No deletion.

### Store

`Draft` → `Active` → `Suspended` / `Paused` per [ADR-0003](../../decisions/0003-store-lifecycle-and-state-machine.md). Full state machine in §5.8 of [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) and the ADR.

Wire-state projection (handled by the Bridge):
| Domain state | Wire signal |
|---|---|
| `Draft` | Catalog not published |
| `Active` | Catalog published with `isActive: true` |
| `Paused` | Catalog published with `isActive: false` |
| `Suspended` | Catalog published with `isActive: false` |

(Beckn v2 doesn't distinguish owner-pause from platform-suspend at the wire; the distinction is retained internally.)

### OrganizationMember

| From → To | Actor / Trigger |
|---|---|
| (new) → Active (Owner) | At Org creation |
| (new) → Active (Member) | Via Org Invitation acceptance |
| Active (Member) → Removed | Org Owner; cascades: removes their `StoreAdminAssignment`s in this Org |
| Active (Owner) ↔ Active (Member) | Via explicit ownership-transfer flow (two-party confirmation) |

The Org Owner role can **only** transition via the explicit transfer flow (`Org.InitiateOwnershipTransfer` + `Org.AcceptOwnershipTransfer`).

### OrganizationInvitation

`Pending` → `Accepted` | `Declined` | `Revoked` | `Expired`. One-way transitions. Reconciliation rules per ADR-0010.

### StoreAdminAssignment

`Active` → `Removed` (by Org Owner, or cascaded when the user is removed from the Org or Disabled).

## Authorization (cross-cuts every context)

Per ADR-0002 + ADR-0016 + ADR-0018, the system has three tiers:

- **System Admin** — meta-admins, platform-wide.
- **Platform-scoped** — operators (support, compliance) acting across tenants per capability.
- **Tenant-scoped** — Org-bound. Within Tenant-scope, two sub-roles:
  - **Org Owner** — full authority over the Org and all stores within.
  - **Store Admin** — administrative access to specific stores via `StoreAdminAssignment`.
  - Baseline **Org Member** (no Store assignments): only `org.view`.

Authorization is action-level (`product.publish`, `voucher.disable`, `org.invite_member`, etc.). Every use case begins with `requireCapability(name, scope)`. See [§5.1](../05-cross-cutting.md) (forthcoming) and [ADR-0016](../../decisions/0016-authorization-details.md).

## Boundary contracts (highlights)

### Use cases exposed (Application Layer)

**Organization management:**
- `Org.Create`, `Org.Update`, `Org.GetById`
- `Org.Suspend`, `Org.Reactivate` (platform-scoped)
- `Org.InitiateOwnershipTransfer`, `Org.AcceptOwnershipTransfer`, `Org.RevokeOwnershipTransfer`

**Member management:**
- `Org.InviteMember`, `Org.AcceptInvitation`, `Org.DeclineInvitation`, `Org.RevokeInvitation`
- `Org.RemoveMember`

**Store administration:**
- `Org.CreateStore` (creates a Store in `Draft` state in the given Org)
- `Org.AssignStoreAdmin`, `Org.UnassignStoreAdmin`

**Store lifecycle (per ADR-0003):**
- `Store.SubmitForActivation`, `Store.Activate` (platform), `Store.Suspend` (platform), `Store.Pause` (owner), `Store.Unpause` (owner)

**Manual catalog republication (per ADR-0019):**
- `Store.RequestRepublish(store_id)` — Store Admin / Org Owner triggers republish of a store
- `Org.RequestRepublishAll(org_id)` — Org Owner triggers republish of all stores in the Org

**Read-side:**
- `Org.ListMyOrganizations(user_id)` — drives Org switcher UI
- `Org.ListMembers`, `Org.ListStores`, `Org.ListStoreAdmins`

Full list in [`design/tenancy.md`](../../design/tenancy.md).

### Domain events emitted

Store lifecycle: `tenancy.store_created`, `tenancy.store_submitted_for_activation`, `tenancy.store_status_changed`.

Organizations: `tenancy.organization_created`, `tenancy.organization_updated`, `tenancy.organization_suspended`, `tenancy.organization_reactivated`, `tenancy.organization_member_added`, `tenancy.organization_member_removed`, `tenancy.organization_ownership_transfer_initiated`, `tenancy.organization_ownership_transferred`, `tenancy.organization_ownership_transfer_revoked`, `tenancy.store_admin_assigned`, `tenancy.store_admin_unassigned`.

Invitations: `tenancy.invitation_created`, `tenancy.invitation_accepted`, `tenancy.invitation_declined`, `tenancy.invitation_revoked`, `tenancy.invitation_expired`.

Manual republication: `tenancy.store_republish_requested`, `tenancy.organization_republish_requested`.

Full registry: [`design/events.md`](../../design/events.md).

## Notable rules / invariants

- **Exactly one** `OrganizationMember` with `role = Owner` and `status = Active` per Organization, at all times.
- A **Store's `organization_id` is immutable** — no cross-Org transfer in v1.
- A **`StoreAdminAssignment`** can only target a user who is an Active `OrganizationMember` of the Store's Org.
- The **Org Owner does not need** a `StoreAdminAssignment` for any store — implicit access.
- **Organization slug is unique within the platform**; **Store slug is unique within its Organization** (not platform-wide).
- The **Org Owner cannot be removed** via `org.remove_member` — must first transfer ownership.
- **Invitations are Org-level only** (per ADR-0018); Store Admin status is a direct assignment, not an invitation.

## Out of scope (v1)

- **Cross-Org store transfer** — Store's `organization_id` is immutable.
- **Per-Store granular roles** (e.g., "catalog editor but not pricing") — would extend the matrix; future.
- **Org-level shared catalog templates** — future.
- **Org-wide promotions / vouchers** — vouchers remain store-scoped (per ADR-0007).
- **Multi-store cart / unified buyer experience across an Org** — not applicable; Bridge sees only individual stores.
